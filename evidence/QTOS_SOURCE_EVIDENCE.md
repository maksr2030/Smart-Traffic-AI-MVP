## Provenance rule

The identifiers `QTOS-01` through `QTOS-25` are canonical identifiers for the documented Quantum Traffic Orchestration System sub-innovation. They are not historical legacy numbers and must not be used to fill the reserved historical gap 11-199.

The source artifact is retained as evidence of the documented QTOS package. A capability being documented in this artifact does not by itself establish production deployment, field validation, live government integration, or measured quantum advantage.
